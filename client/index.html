BOWMAN 2

Drag back. Aim. Outshoot your rival.

How to play
  • Drag back anywhere, like a slingshot, then let go. The further you pull, the harder the shot.
  • Head 50 · body 25 · legs 15. You both start with 100.
  • Wind (top centre) bends every arrow. It changes each turn.
  • You get 25 seconds a turn. Miss three turns in a row and you forfeit.
